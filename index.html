Interior design and visualisation shaped by atmosphere, materiality, light, and the way a space is lived.

Khoury Design is a London and Bolzano collaboration between Stephan and Emilie Khoury. The studio works across interior design and architectural visualisation for residential clients, with a strong focus on material warmth, spatial calm, and the emotional reality of daily life in a home. We are interested in how a room feels to inhabit, not only how it reads in a photograph.

03 The Studio

Stephan
Khoury

Visualisation and Concept, London

Over a decade of architectural and interior visualisation. Trained in digital arts at Chapman University, California, and in psychology at the University of Glasgow. The psychology background is not decorative. It informs how the studio reads the emotional register of a space: the effect of light temperature, the nervous system response to proportion, the difference between a room that calms and one that unsettles.

3ds Max, Cinema 4D, Corona, V-Ray, Unreal Engine 5, ZBrush, Substance Painter

Emilie
Khoury

Interior Design, Bolzano

Interior design with a foundation in fashion styling and advanced training in Milan. Emilie brings material intelligence to the studio: the tactile composition of a room, the layering of textile and finish, the softness and warmth that makes a space feel genuinely inhabited rather than staged.

Residential interiors, material palette, furnishings and styling, client-facing design communication

Atmosphere

How a room is entered, inhabited, and remembered. The emotional tone is designed before the material palette.

Materiality

Stone, timber, textile, brass. What a space offers the hand as much as the eye.

Light

Daylight and artificial light studied together. Temperature, direction, shadow: the primary tools of spatial mood.

Wellbeing

The psychological reality of how a space is lived. Calm, grounded, restorative: designed into the structure, not applied after.
