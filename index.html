Khoury Design combines interior design and architectural visualisation with a strong focus on atmosphere, materiality, light, and wellbeing.

Working between London and Bolzano, the studio approaches residential space as both a visual composition and an emotional environment — shaped by the psychological reality of how a space is lived and felt.

Studio

Khoury Design brings together two complementary disciplines. Stephan Khoury's background in digital arts, classical culture, and psychology informs the studio's approach to visual atmosphere, spatial storytelling, and the emotional effect of light and form. Emilie Khoury's training in fashion styling and interior design in Milan brings a refined understanding of materiality, composition, furnishings, and the lived texture of residential space.

Together, the studio is interested not only in how spaces look, but in how they regulate, soften, energise, and support daily life — through light, layout, texture, and a strong sensitivity to the emotional tone of interiors.

Stephan

Stephan's background spans digital arts at Chapman University, classical culture and archaeology, and an MSc in psychology from the University of Glasgow. Atmosphere

How a room is entered, inhabited, and remembered. The emotional tone is designed before the material palette.

Materiality

Stone, timber, textile, brass. What a space offers the hand as much as the eye.

Light

Daylight and artificial light studied together. Temperature, direction, shadow: the primary tools of spatial mood.

Wellbeing

Calm, grounded, restorative: qualities designed into the structure of a space, not applied after the fact.
